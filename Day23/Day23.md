# Day 23: Customer & MVP Blueprint

**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Project:** AI-Powered Career Platform for College Students (India)
**Stage:** Concept / early MVP
**Status:** 🟡 Promising but Unvalidated

---

## Goal for today

Turn the Startup Validation Report (58/100, Conditional Go) into a concrete **Customer & MVP Blueprint**: who exactly I'm building for, what to build first, and how to test demand in 30 days.

## What I did

- Extracted the idea, problem, target customers, competitors and validation insights from the validation report.
- Defined the **beachhead ICP**: 2nd/3rd-year CSE/IT/AIML students at tier-2/3 colleges.
- Defined the **B2B buyer**: Training & Placement Officers (TPOs) at private engineering colleges with 300+ CS students.
- Mapped the top 10 customer pain points, the customer journey, objections and buying triggers.
- Decided the MVP scope, wrote the MoSCoW prioritization and a pricing hypothesis.
- Built a 30-day plan and a top-10 founder action list.
- Generated a 4-page PDF report: `Customer_MVP_Blueprint.pdf`.

## Key decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wedge | "Am I job-ready for role X?" Job-Readiness Check | Roadmaps alone are what a free LLM does in one prompt |
| First build | Concierge-style pilot (manual plus LLM assist) for 25-30 students | Validate demand before writing more product code |
| Role track | Pick **one** (SDE intern or AI/ML intern) | Narrow wedge, easier to benchmark and market |
| Monetization | Free core; pay for reviews, mocks, credentials; colleges pay for analytics | Addresses "ChatGPT is free" and "I won't pay" objections |
| Long-term moat | Verified progress data and proof-of-work | The one thing a generic LLM cannot copy |

## What NOT to build yet

- Native mobile app
- AI mock-interview engine
- Mentor marketplace
- Full college dashboard (use a summary sheet first)
- Job board / integrations
- Multi-role roadmaps

## Biggest risk

**Zero primary evidence.** The problem is well supported by secondary data (employability 56.35%, graduate unemployment ~11.2%), but there are no interviews or surveys yet. Willingness to pay, retention and "better than ChatGPT" are all still assumptions.

## 30-day success criteria

| Metric | GO | PIVOT | NO-GO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interviews done | 30+ | 20-29 | < 15 |
| Students rating problem 7+/10 | 60%+ | 40-59% | < 40% |
| Pilot 4-week retention | 40%+ | 20-39% | < 20% |
| Pay or pre-commit | 8%+ or 3+ colleges | 3-7% | < 3% |
| Side-by-side vs ChatGPT | Mine preferred | Split | ChatGPT preferred |

## Learnings

- Gaps 1-2 in the report (unified view, adaptive advice) are **features**; gaps 3-4 (verified proof-of-work, college analytics) are the **businesses**.
- India-only B2C at ~INR 1,500/student is a good business but not venture-scale, so ARPU or a B2B layer has to grow.
- The critical moment is **activation to habit**: no visible improvement within 7 days means churn.
