Sources

    How this was researched
    • Scope. The four phones you chose, as the base model of each: iPhone 16 (128GB), Galaxy S25 (128GB), Pixel 9 (128GB), OnePlus 13 (256GB). Newer successors exist as of October 2026 (iPhone 17, Galaxy S26, Pixel 10, OnePlus 15) and are not included.
    • Method. Figures were read from the pages listed under Sources on 29 October 2026, as shown in search results for each page. Nothing was measured by me and no figure was invented. Where a value is missing for a phone, I did not substitute one.
    • Price. US launch MSRP for the base model. Live price widgets vary widely by seller and region (GSMArena showed the Galaxy S25 at $380.63 in one snapshot and $674.99 in another), so MSRP is the only consistent comparison. Check today’s street price before buying.
    • Battery. The endurance figure is the EU energy-label rating as listed on each GSMArena spec page, which is the only battery metric I found for all four phones. GSMArena’s own Active Use scores were found for only some phones, so I left them out.
    • Charging. Rated wired charging power in watts. Real charging time also depends on the charger and battery size. Where available, sourced context is in the data table notes.
    • Performance. Geekbench 6 multi-core. This is a synthetic benchmark and not a measure of everyday speed.
    • Camera. PhoneArena Camera Score, because it was the only test I found that covers all four phones on one scale. DXOMARK publishes a camera score for the iPhone 16 only among the four (I found none for the other three).
    • Scoring. Each criterion is scaled 0 to 100 across the four phones (price is inverted so cheaper scores higher). Your weights then average the five scores. Camera scores sit within about 3 points of each other, so camera rarely changes the ranking.

    Conflicts I had to resolve

    • iPhone 16 charging: GSMArena’s spec page lists 25W, while its review measured about 30W peak and Apple publishes no rating. I used 25W so it is on the same “listed rating” basis as the others, and flagged it as an estimate.
    • OnePlus 13 charging: GSMArena lists 100W (global model, 0 to 100% in 36 minutes). Front Desk Review states the US model is rated 80W. I used 100W and flagged it. At 80W it would still lead on charging.
    • OnePlus 13 Geekbench: NanoReview shows 8,999, Beebom 9,467 and another aggregator 9,278. I did not retrieve a GSMArena figure for this phone, so I used the lowest (8,999) and flagged it as cross-source.
    • iPhone 16 camera: PhoneArena gives 149 and DXOMARK gives 147. These are different scales, so I used PhoneArena for all four and mention DXOMARK only as corroboration.
    • Pixel 9 price: The $799 figure comes from an aggregator that cites GSMArena rather than from Google’s own page. I found no conflicting MSRP.
