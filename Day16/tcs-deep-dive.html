Company Overview

TCS, founded in 1968, is India's largest IT services company and the flagship of the Tata Group. It provides IT services, consulting, and business solutions across BFSI, Manufacturing, Life Sciences, Retail, and other sectors, with North America as its largest market.

In Q2 FY26, consolidated revenue was ₹65,799 Cr (up 2.4% YoY) with net profit of ₹12,131 Cr. Standalone FY26 (Mar-26) full-year revenue was ₹2,20,938 Cr with operating margin around 28%. [Source: Screener.in, company filings]
