Company Overview

Infosys is India's second-largest IT services company, providing consulting, technology, outsourcing and digital transformation services globally, with a strong presence in cloud, AI-led analytics, and enterprise modernization.

TTM consolidated revenue stands at ≈₹1,84,582 Cr with net profit of ≈₹30,325 Cr (net margin ≈16.1%). The company carries effectively zero debt. [Source: ValueResearch peer comparison, 22 Sep 2026]
