# Day 36: Explore Your Thinking Patterns

**Today's topic:** Discover how you naturally approach decisions and uncertainty.

> Educational and reflective only. This is not a diagnosis or a clinical assessment. The aim is curiosity about yourself, not labels.

---

## 1. Why this matters

Everyone has habitual ways of handling choices and the unknown. Noticing yours helps you:

- Understand why some decisions feel easy and others feel heavy.
- Spot when a strength is being overused.
- Choose a different approach on purpose when a situation calls for it.

Most people use a mix of styles and shift between them depending on mood, stakes and energy.

---

## 2. Five common thinking tendencies

| Tendency | You often... | A strength | A gentle watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Analytical Thinker** | look for facts, structure and clear criteria | Clarity and logic | Missing how a choice feels |
| **Emotional Intuitive** | tune in to feelings, tone and people | Empathy and gut sense | Skipping concrete facts |
| **Overthinking Loop Style** | revisit a situation, searching for the perfect angle | Thoroughness and care | Stalling or tiring yourself out |
| **Action-First Decision Maker** | move quickly and learn by doing | Momentum and adaptability | Acting before a useful pause |
| **Balanced Reflective Thinker** | blend feeling, thinking and action, then pause | Flexibility | Balance turning into hesitation |

---

## 3. Warm-up (5 minutes)

Think of a decision you made in the last month. Write short answers:

1. What was the situation?
2. What was the very first thing I did or thought?
3. Which of the five tendencies does that resemble?
4. How did it turn out, and how did I feel afterward?

## 6. Reflection journal prompts

- Which tendency surprised me most, and why?
- Did my results change between Calm and Stress Mode? What might that suggest?
- When has my default style helped me recently?
- When has it gotten in my way?
- What is one small experiment I could try this week with a different approach?

---

## 7. One small experiment for today

Pick **one** decision today, however small, and deliberately use a style you use less often.

- Usually analytical? Check in with how each option feels.
- Usually intuitive? Write down two facts before choosing.
- Usually overthinking? Set a 10-minute limit, then decide.
- Usually action-first? Pause for one slow breath and one question: "What am I missing?"
- Usually balanced? Notice whether you can commit once you've reflected.
