Day 10 — AI-Powered Personal Portfolio Website

🚀 Overview

For Day 10 of my 60-Day Claude AI Challenge, I used Claude to transform my resume and professional profile into a modern, recruiter-friendly personal portfolio website.

The website is designed around my career direction as a Software Engineer with a focus on AI/ML and Computer Vision, while highlighting my projects, internships, certifications, technical skills, and achievements.

The goal was to build a complete personal portfolio website that:

Represents my professional profile accurately

Converts resume information into web-friendly content

Highlights my AI/ML and software engineering projects

Makes my achievements and certifications easy to find

Provides recruiters with a quick overview of my skills

Creates a professional online presence beyond a traditional resume

I provided Claude with a structured portfolio-development prompt containing:

Personal information

Professional title

Skills

Projects

Experience

Achievements

Responsive and SEO requirements

Claude then generated the complete single-file portfolio implementation.

The resume served as the primary source for the website's professional content, allowing the portfolio to remain aligned with my actual academic background, internships, projects, skills, and achievements.

💡 Key Learning

This task helped me understand how AI can support personal branding as well as software development.

Instead of treating a resume and portfolio as completely separate assets, I learned how structured career information can be transformed into a consistent digital identity.

I also learned that better prompts produce better results when they clearly define:

Who the website represents

What information must be included

Who the target audience is

What technologies should be used

What the visual style should be

What interactions the website should support
