# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Day
Day 3 of 60 Days Claude Challenge

## Topic
Role-Based Prompting

## What I Learned

Today I learned how assigning a specific role or persona to an AI can change the way it responds. Different roles provide different perspectives, expertise, priorities, and styles of answering.

I tested the same question using three approaches:
1. No assigned role
2. Founder persona
3. Developer persona

## Question

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup as a college student?

## Prompt 1 - No Role

How can I build a successful AI-powered startup as a college student?

## Prompt 2 - Founder Persona

Act as an experienced startup founder who has built and scaled AI companies. Answer this question: How can I build a successful AI-powered startup as a college student? Give practical advice on identifying a problem, validating the idea, building an MVP, getting users, and scaling.

## Prompt 3 - Developer Persona

Act as a senior AI/software developer. Answer this question: How can I build a successful AI-powered startup as a college student? Focus on choosing the technology stack, building the MVP, using AI/ML effectively, deployment, testing, and development best practices.

## Comparison

The response without a role was more general and balanced.

The Founder persona focused more on business strategy, customer problems, validation, MVP development, users, and scaling.

The Developer persona focused more on technical implementation, AI/ML technologies, development, testing, deployment, and the technology stack.

## Key Takeaway

Role-Based Prompting helps make AI responses more relevant to a particular situation. By assigning a role, I can guide the AI to respond from a specific professional perspective.
